# The Living Wisdom School — logic, story, and why it is held back

**Status: built, complete, and deliberately not on the live site (28 Aug 2026).**

This file is the durable record. The page files are the artefact; this is the
reasoning, the argument, and the decisions behind them — written so that the work
survives without anyone having to read HTML, and so that whoever picks it up next does
not have to re-derive what was already settled.

---

## 1. What happened, in order

**Source.** *Living Wisdom School — Updated Working Overview for Roman, Kelly &
Joseph*, Paititi Institute, August 2026 (PDF, internal). Twenty-two sections
describing an umbrella educational ecosystem: the Evolutionary Blueprint as its map,
five lineage streams, a four-rite passage path, layered online learning, physical hubs,

part of the school* — is the only thing that licensed putting anything on the site

a "Two Schools, One Movement" block on `/initiatives#culture-learning`, a sixth flow
line in `#living-system`, a paragraph on `/online-courses`, and a "Rites of Passage

## 2. The story the page tells

Eight movements. This is the argument in prose; the page is this argument with
pictures.

**The idea.** The Living Wisdom School is an educational, experiential and community
ecosystem being built at Paititi to preserve, transmit and embody humanity's living
wisdom traditions. It is not an online academy, a retreat program, a media project or a
collection of teachings, though it touches all four. Its purpose is to hold a bridge
between ancestral and contemplative traditions, modern psychology, direct experiential
practice, rites of passage, Indigenous cultural preservation, ecological
responsibility, community, and service. The question underneath it is not how to
*preserve* ancient wisdom — wisdom kept safe is wisdom that has stopped moving — but
how to create conditions in which it stays alive, understandable, embodied, ethically
transmitted, and relevant to how people actually live. *A container for living wisdom,
rather than a library of spiritual information.*

**Why now, part one: access without a container.** People have more access to teachings
than at any point in history — Tibetan Buddhism, Amazonian cosmology, Qigong, Jungian
psychology, Indigenous elders, meditation and neuroscience inside a single week. Access
is not transformation. What is missing is everything that historically surrounded a
teaching: worldview, lineage, a real relationship with a teacher, disciplined practice,
ethical orientation, community, cultural context, rites of passage, relationship with
land, integration over time, responsibility. Without that container people accumulate
experiences, teachers and traditions instead of changing.

**Why now, part two: organic intelligence.** As more of life is mediated by devices,
algorithms and synthetic information, direct relationship with body, nature, intuition,
silence and the living world becomes less ordinary. The school will use technology —
for teaching, translation, accessibility, reach. The concern was never technology. It
is forgetting organic intelligence. *Technology should serve consciousness. It should
not become a substitute for it.* This is the strongest and most contemporary argument
in the whole document, and it is why the page gives it a chapter of its own rather than
a line.

**The streams.** At the centre is not a single tradition but a question about our true
nature. Around it sit distinct living streams — **Amazonian** (living nature, dreams,
healing, ceremonial context, plant relationships, reciprocity, remembrance),
**Andean** (ayni, Pachamama and the Apus, offering, pilgrimage, embodied prayer),
**Tibetan** (mind training, compassion, subtle body, impermanence and
emptiness-awareness, where transmission is appropriate and properly held), **Daoist**
(naturalness, Qi, internal cultivation, Five Elements, microcosm and macrocosm), and
**depth psychology** (dreams, shadow, projection, archetypes, individuation, and
integrating extraordinary experience back into ordinary life). The school does not
claim they teach the same thing and is not an attempt to assemble a synthetic universal
religion. Each stays rooted in its own integrity.

**The map underneath.** The Evolutionary Blueprint is the shared frame: how identity is
constructed out of family, biology, culture and conditioning; how a call or a crisis
disrupts the self that seemed sufficient; the descent into what has been denied or
projected; the moments in which something more fundamental is directly recognised; the
long work of integration; and the turn outward into reciprocity and service. The map,
not the destination. It does not replace any tradition — it gives students a common
frame for what they meet through them.

**Taught by the people who hold it.** Wherever possible a living tradition should be
represented by its own teachers, elders, practitioners and lineage holders. The school
should not mainly speak *about* Indigenous or contemplative traditions; it should
create the conditions in which their own representatives speak for themselves. Where
interpretation is needed, its job is not to explain a tradition on behalf of the people
who carry it, but to give contemporary students enough context to genuinely hear what
those teachers are saying.

**How the learning happens.** Three things have to move together: **understanding**
(worldview, philosophy, mythology, psychology, cosmology, cultural context),
**practice** (breathwork, meditation, qigong, dreamwork, movement, contemplative
inquiry, nature-based practice) and **initiation** (experiences that move a person
outside habitual structures and ask something deeper to become embodied). Understanding
without practice stays intellectual. Practice without understanding becomes mechanical.
Initiation without either becomes one more extraordinary experience to collect. Around
these sit four passages — Amazon, Andes, deep rainforest, Himalayas — and, crucially,
*the retreats are not the school*: between the passages is where most of it happens, in
conflict, grief, family, work and service, when nothing extraordinary is going on.

**Two schools, one movement.** This is the pivot, and the most important structural
idea in the whole document. Keeping wisdom alive asks two things at once. The
**ancestral schools** face inward: they help elders teach their own language, songs,
ceremony and plant knowledge directly to the children of their own communities, in
their own territory and on their own terms — the Yahua Ancestral School first, a Q'ero
one in development. The **Living Wisdom School** faces outward: the body of study
through which people from anywhere can meet these traditions honestly, and it exists
because interest from outside is going to arrive whether or not there is an ethical way
to receive it. What participants contribute sustains the ancestral schools; the
ancestral schools keep alive the traditions the school exists to introduce. Neither
stands on its own.

**Built in the open.** There is no reason to wait until the school is finished before
speaking about it. The building of it is part of it — the inspiration, the Indigenous
relationships, Paititi's history, the ancestral schools, the conservation work, the
evolving curriculum, and the questions still being argued over. What gets published
includes the difficulties, not only the milestones. Trust is built by showing results:
public milestones, photographs and video, construction progress, completed programs,
annual impact and financial reporting — what was raised, what was spent, what was
accomplished, what comes next.

## 3. The editorial logic

Six rules the copy follows. They are not stylistic; each one is load-bearing, and
breaking one creates a specific, identifiable problem.

**1. Everything forward-looking is written in the present continuous.** "Being built",
"taking shape", "is being refined", "is being decided". The school does not exist as a
bookable thing. The moment a sentence says it *does*, the page becomes a prospectus for
something nobody can buy, and every other claim on the site gets read with more
suspicion.

**2. No stage count for the Evolutionary Blueprint.** See §5. The page describes the
Blueprint's movements in prose and names no number.

**3. No individual is named as faculty — and the page says why.** The absence is
explained in a paragraph rather than left as a hole: who will teach is being decided
through relationships that already exist and relationships still being formed,
including agreement about what may be recorded and taught, and *"announcing one before
those agreements are real would be exactly the mistake this principle exists to
prevent."* This turns the biggest gap in the page into the clearest demonstration of
its ethics. It should not be replaced with a placeholder faculty grid.

**4. The rite ordering is a map, not a gate.** The source document frames Rite IV as
being for people who have "established greater grounding through practice and earlier
experiences" — a prerequisite. The Himalayan Pilgrimage takes open registration with no
prerequisite. So the copy says the ordering "describes how they relate to one another
rather than a set of requirements: each is open on its own terms". If prerequisites
ever become real, **that sentence changes first, and registration changes with it.**

**5. Status markers on the rites are not decoration.** Rites I and IV say "Running
now" and link to `/retreats`. Rites II and III say "In development" and "A longer
horizon", and Rite III additionally states its preconditions — Indigenous leadership,
participant selection, health screening, safety planning. A wilderness initiation that
reads as bookable is a safety problem, not a copy problem.

**6. No dates.** The 2027 launch and the 2028–2030 sequence stay internal. Published
roadmaps age badly, and this one has no committed resourcing behind it.

## 4. The naming collision

"Living Wisdom" now means three different things, and two of them predate the document:

| Sense | Where it lives | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Living Wisdom **Center** | `SiteHeader.dc.html` announcement bar, `InitiativeYagua.dc.html` + its meta description | a **building** — the maloca and campus at the Yahua ancestral school, and the current donation ask |
| Living Wisdom **Courses** | `OnlineCourses.dc.html`, `Home.dc.html`, `DiscoverPaititi.dc.html` | the four Retreat Guru courses |
| Living Wisdom **School** | the source document, and the held-back page | the umbrella containing retreats, courses, mentorship |

Spanish carries it identically: *Centro de Sabiduría Viva* / *Cursos de Sabiduría Viva*
/ *Escuela de Sabiduría Viva*.

**Why it matters:** a donor who reads the announcement bar and then a school page cannot
tell which one their money builds. The page's "note on names" paragraph in Two Schools
is currently the only place on the site where the Center and the School are told apart
— it is a patch over an unmade decision, not the decision.

**Proposed, pending Roman:** Center stays the Yahua building; School is the umbrella;
"Living Wisdom Courses" as a section heading retires to plain "Courses" once the school
is public. Not applied — it touches six files across two languages.

## 5. The Evolutionary Blueprint exists in two versions

The source document's Blueprint has **seven** movements: Conditioning → The Call →
Descent → Remembrance → Integration → Reciprocity → Service.

The Blueprint course actually sold on `/online-courses` has **five** chapters: the call
to evolve, crossing the line of no return, remembering the infinite, finding the way
through the patterns of evolution, emerging from the dark night of the soul.

Same framework, earlier version. This is consistent with the document's own 2027 line,
"refine and relaunch the Evolutionary Blueprint" — and the additions are meaningful
rather than cosmetic: **Conditioning** at the front and **Reciprocity** and **Service**
at the back are exactly the turn from personal development toward the nonprofit work.

**Consequence: no page may state a stage count until Roman settles it**, or the school
copy and the course card contradict each other in public. The held-back page names
neither number and says the Blueprint "is being refined for the school; an earlier
version is already taught as a course", which is true either way.

## 6. What is deliberately absent

- **Kelly and Joseph by name.** The Team page is Roman-only by his own 11 Aug decision.
  No bios, no photos, no consent. The page says nothing about who staffs the school.
- **Named elders or teachers.** §19 of the source says the council should grow through
  real relationships rather than be assembled for appearance; a website faculty grid is
  the same mistake in another form.
- **Membership tiers** (Participant / Practitioner / Steward / Mentor), **the hub list**
  (Peru, Bay Area, Korea, Thailand, Nepal) and **the layered-learning ladder**
  (Seed / Teaching / Deep Transmission / Reflection / Practice / Dialogue / Life
  Application). All are structure describing how the school *would* run, and it does
  not run yet.
- **Anything resembling enrollment.** No prices, no waitlist, no "apply". The single
  forward-looking call is a link to `/contact`.
- **The source document itself.** Addressed to three named people, reads as internal. A
  public-facing version would be a separate artefact, in the manner of the Living
  Vision mind-map PDF.

## 7. What exists, and how to bring it back

**In the repo, complete:**

- `LivingWisdomSchool.dc.html` and `LivingWisdomSchool.es.dc.html` — hero, sticky
  chapter subnav, six chapters (The Idea · Why Now · The Streams · Living Teachers ·
  The Path · Two Schools) and a closing "Being Built in the Open".
- Composed, not transcribed. Like Distance Healing, this page has no live counterpart
  to measure, so everything below the hero is ordinary flow inside a 1000px measure
  rather than pinned fluid-grid rows. Verified at 1440, 1190 and 390px in both trees:
  no horizontal scroll, nothing escaping the viewport.
- Imagery is reused from elsewhere on the site: the Q'ero expedition group (hero), the
  fractal flower and golden spiral, the Andean embrace, the candlelit maloca circle.
  If the school carries the next phase of the story it should eventually get its own.

**Held back by five reversible switches:**

1. `.assetsignore` — both files excluded from `wrangler deploy`.
2. `_redirects` — both clean-URL lines commented out.
3. `tools/i18n_pairs.json` — the pair removed, so `apply_hreflang.py` keeps both pages
   out of `sitemap.xml` and out of each other's hreflang.
4. `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` on both pages.
5. Nav links removed from `SiteHeader.dc.html` and `SiteHeader.es.dc.html` (desktop
   flyout and mobile menu, both languages).

Reversing all five is written at the top of `LivingWisdomSchool.dc.html`. To preview it
meanwhile, serve the repo and open `/LivingWisdomSchool.dc.html` directly — the clean
URL is off, the file is not.

**Also recoverable, when the weave-in is wanted:** the 27 Aug grafts, reverted on
28 Aug. `/retreats` and `/online-courses` are in commit `a4e77f4`; the `/initiatives`
"Two Schools" block and sixth flow line are in the 28 Aug revert commit. When they go
back they should point at `/living-wisdom-school` rather than at
`/initiatives#culture-learning`, and `/retreats` needs its `#amazon-immersion` and
`#himalayan-pilgrimage` section ids restored for the rite cards to link to.

## 8. Open for Roman

The first three block any further building. The rest are non-blocking.

1. **The naming collision** (§4) — Center vs Courses vs School.
2. **Blueprint stage count** (§5) — the document's seven, or the course's five?
3. **Rite prerequisites** — map or gate? Decides whether the Himalayan registration
   stays open to anyone.
4. **Is the page the right shape?** Six chapters, one page, this order, this length.
   "A whole new section" might mean several pages; splitting later is cheap, knowing
   now is cheaper.
5. **When does it become visible, and how?** This is now the live question — the page
   exists and is waiting on a decision about its introduction.
6. **How public is the roadmap?** Whether "2027" may appear on the site at all.
7. **Kelly and Joseph** — named publicly as part of this, or not yet? If yes: bios,
   photos, their own sign-off, and it reopens the Roman-only Team page decision.
8. **Announcement bar** — it currently sells the Yahua Center, and two campaigns are
   already open. Does the school get its own moment there, or stay out of it?
9. **Rite II (Andes)** — is the horseback pilgrimage real enough to name as in
   development, or should it come off until there is a date?
10. **Imagery** — does the school get its own photography?
