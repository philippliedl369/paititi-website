Discover Paititi

We are a non-profit organization founded in 2010, dedicated to Mother Earth and the Infinite Human Potential.

“Paititi”, in Quechua, is an enlightened realm manifested through the awakening of our shared human heart.

As humans, we are inexorably linked to the earth and each other. The intercultural bridges our organization is dedicated to developing are based on the essential nature of reciprocity and interconnectedness that this planet is sustained by. As part of this magnificent symbiotic organism, we are dedicated to co-creating the possibility of a meaningful life and a beautiful world for our children and future generations.

Dedication to self-realization of highest potential, while humbly sharing it with each other for the greatest benefit of all, reflects the symbiotic quality of Nature’s organic intelligence we are all a part of. This is a core value of Paititi and informs the essence of all our projects, such as healing serious health conditions, consciousness transformation, land stewardship, community gardening and permaculture, free natural medicine campaigns for rural communities or social development initiatives. Being a responsible, hearted-centered human being is the key to the blossoming of life on our planet.

Through the awakened spirit of the individual comes the greatest potential for the transformation of the planet. The Paititi Institute for the Preservation of Ecology and Indigenous Culture is committed to embodying this paradigm shift and demonstrating what is possible through ordinary, grassroots human efforts, in service to Mother Nature and the infinite human potential.

The founding vision of our organization is to be a conduit for innermost heart values to manifest into material realm. Throughout all our programs and initiatives, our efforts focus on supporting a society rooted in true values. In recognition of timeless, essential human values, ancestral wisdom is translated into practical and relevant modern circumstances

Paititi’s Initiatives Fall Into 5 Key Areas:

Preserving & Integrating of Ancestral Wisdom

The cumulative wisdom of humanity containing the essential methods and insights necessary to liberate from the worst suffering is available today through the living millennial lineages from around the world. This wisdom is geared towards an awakening of human organism to the unconditional nature of existence beyond all cultural, societal and personal differences and can only be honored through each individual’s inner reconciliation. In our organization the living wisdom lineages of Tibet, The Amazon and The Andes are applied in a direct and experiential way encouraging each individual to face themselves, find inner peace and remember what it is to be a happy and healthy human being. These are “living wisdom traditions” because it is through each individual bringing this wisdom into daily life that we find genuine transformation of our experience beyond conceptual knowledge.

Consciousness Transformation & Evolutionary Healing

We provide a supportive environment through retreats, courses and community living where individuals can safely immerse in the evolutionary journey of self-realization. The skillful means and rites of passage cultivated within such an intentional space allow individuals to realize their unique gifts, qualities, and talents, to be embodied in every-day community living. As we collectively move through powerful rites of passage we unearth our creative potential and learn to embody and share our unique gifts in community.

Restoring & Maintaining Health through Traditional & Natural Methods

We utilize indigenous medicine practices and natural healing modalities from around the world to address sickness and disease on a variety of levels, from psycho-spiritual to somatic. In our experience, this approach to healing addresses the root cause of diseases and includes the healing of each individual as a whole rather than merely targeting symptoms. Today, more than any other time in history, we believe that the world needs viable models for this type of comprehensive and evolutionary healing.

Earth Stewardship via Eco-regenerative Community Living

We are the stewards of a 4000 acre nature reserve and sanctuary where our land based community and retreat center is based. Our center in Peru is a community platform where we can practice to embody a holistic model for the world. We work with Permacutlure and local ancestral wisdom to support us in stewarding this land in the most conscious way possible.

Conservation & Regeneration of Natural Resources

We are dedicated to the preservation and restoration of our precious natural resources. We work with permaculture and natural farming techniques creating vibrant, local food economies. Our food forests and gardens serve as an educational model and and experiential learning group of how to live in harmonious relationship with Pachamama (Mother Earth). We also work to support local Peruvian communities to find fair trade markets for ecological & regenerative products.

How do we Embody our highest potential and live in harmony with each other and the planet?

From the awakened spirit of the individual comes the greatest potential for the transformation of the planet.

We believe that the only way forward is to be the change. Our work as an institute is rooted in our efforts to embody this and to share tools for others to do the same. Through the practical application of the ancient wisdom of humanity within the modern lives, we build bridges for the physical, emotional, and spiritual healing of our ecology from the inside and out.

In this natural way, we channel the healing and rejuvenation of the planet herself. Using holistic models based upon our points of focus listed above, we engage as a community to improve social and environmental life via spiritual evolution and maturation on the collective and individual levels. We utilize the planetary wisdom of humanity to become fully conscious and responsible human beings embracing our shared heart.

The ancient, unbroken, and living lineages of direct consciousness transmission are here to help all of us fully realize our true nature in the current turbulent times. By sharing our life experiences with each other we create synergistic networks among all the like-hearted people in the world who are motivated by the freedom and liberation from suffering for all beings.
