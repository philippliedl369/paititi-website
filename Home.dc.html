Our Impact

Over more than two decades of collaboration with Indigenous communities, Paititi Institute has worked to protect biodiversity, strengthen cultural continuity, and improve community well-being through conservation, education, healthcare, and humanitarian initiatives.

Highlights include:

  • 20+ years of partnership with Amazonian and Andean Indigenous communities.

  • 1,516-hectare biocultural reserve protected in the buffer zone of Manu National Park.

  • Officially recognized by Peru's Agency for International Cooperation (APCI) since 2014.

  • 2,000+ people served through free Natural Medicine clinics in collaboration with Indigenous healers and licensed Peruvian physicians.

  • 600+ Indigenous families supported with emergency food relief during the COVID-19 pandemic.

  • Clean water purification system established for the Yahua Nation in the Peruvian Amazon.

  • Supreme Court victory affirming Paititi Institute's ownership of the Paititi Biocultural Reserve and strengthening its long-term protection.

  • Yahua Ancestral School established to preserve Indigenous language, ecological knowledge, arts, ceremonies, and cultural traditions for future generations.

  • 100% of the author's royalties from Beyond Ayahuasca dedicated to supporting Indigenous education and cultural preservation initiatives.
