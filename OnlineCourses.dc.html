Primordial Breathwork

Online home study course series · Sliding-scale contribution

Inspired by the tribal cultures of the Amazonian rainforest, Primordial Breathwork™ works directly with the breath to release what inhibits the organism and reconnect with the source of creative potential. This home-study series focuses on the first portion of the practice: five recorded practice sessions — over four hours of guided breathwork — together with talks on the theory and philosophy that root the practice in its Indigenous origins. The course is ongoing, so you can begin at any time and move at your own pace, returning to each session as the practice deepens.

Alchemy of Immortality Qigong — Andean Art of Being

Online home study course · Sliding-scale contribution

A meeting of Daoist energy cultivation and Andean wisdom teachings: movement, meditation and philosophical study woven into one practice of restoring vital life force. Across three progressive modules — more than three and a half hours of video teachings and active practice sessions — you learn to gather, circulate and settle energy in the body, and to carry the Andean art of being into ordinary daily activity. Self-paced and open-ended, with scholarship applications available so cost is never the barrier to practice.

Ancestral Remembrance

Meditation practice · Taught within the Practical Alchemy Series

The practice of remembrance: remembering the original, uninhibited state of being as a conduit of boundless self-realization. Where breathwork and qigong work through the body and its energy, Ancestral Remembrance Meditation turns attention to what has never been wounded — the ground of being the ancestral lineages point to beneath every technique. It is currently taught as one of the three core Indigenous practices of the Practical Alchemy Series, alongside Primordial Breathwork and the Alchemy of Immortality Qigong.
