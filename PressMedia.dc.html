For journalists

Media enquiries

What we can provide

Interviews with Roman Hanis, co-founder; photography from the reserve and, where the communities have agreed to it, from the Yahua and Q’ero initiatives; footage from the documentary; and review copies of Beyond Ayahuasca.

We ask one thing of anyone writing about the Indigenous communities here: nothing about them is published without their consent, and names and images are theirs to give.

info@paititi-institute.org

Fast facts

  • Founded 2010, on relationships with Amazonian and Andean Indigenous communities begun in 2001.
  • A registered non-profit in Peru (APCI), fiscally sponsored in the United States by Empowerment WORKS, a 501(c)(3).
  • The Paititi Biocultural Reserve — 1,516 hectares of protected Amazonian forest, with title affirmed by the Peruvian Supreme Court.
  • Working alongside the Yahua and Q’ero nations on ancestral education, clean water, community health and food security.
  • 100% of Roman Hanis’ author royalties from Beyond Ayahuasca support the Institute’s Indigenous education and cultural preservation initiatives.
