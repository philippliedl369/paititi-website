Retreats at Paititi are not an escape from ordinary life. They are immersive spaces for remembering what becomes possible when ancestral practice, contemplative inquiry, nature and community are brought together, and for learning to carry that remembrance back into relationship, family, work and service.

The Rites of Passage Path

Our journeys are being drawn together into a longer path — four passages, each asking something different of the person who walks it. The ordering describes how they relate to one another rather than a set of requirements: each is open on its own terms, and two of them are running now.

Rite I — Amazon

Embodying True Nature

The foundational immersion: body, breath, nature, shadow, dream, community, Indigenous worldview, ceremony, and the relationship between healing and self-recognition. Sacred plant practices sit inside a much larger frame of worldview, responsibility and integration rather than standing on their own.

Running now. See the 16-day immersion below ↓

Rite II — Andes

The Hero’s Journey

An extended pilgrimage through Andean landscapes, turning on courage, intention, uncertainty, relationship with the mountains, discipline, offering and embodied prayer.

In development. Not yet open for registration.

Rite III — Deep rainforest

Radical Relationship

A wilderness initiation undertaken with experienced Indigenous guidance. The principle is not survival against nature but the recognition that human life has always depended on relationship with a living ecosystem.

A longer horizon. It would require Indigenous leadership, careful participant selection, health screening and substantial safety planning before it could ever be offered.

Rite IV — Himalayas

Nature of Mind

Pilgrimage, contemplative practice, silence and mind training, alongside encounters with Himalayan teachers and lineages. The Himalayas are not placed above the Indigenous traditions here; they open another face of the same map.

Running now. See the Nepal pilgrimage below ↓

Himalayan Pilgrimage: An Indigenous Wisdom Journey Through Nepal

Join us for a unique collaboration between Paititi Institute and the Karuna Project on a 15-day pilgrimage through the sacred landscapes of Nepal.

This immersive journey weaves together Himalayan trekking, Tibetan Buddhist philosophy, and the indigenous wisdom traditions of the Amazon and Andes. Through Primordial Breathwork, Qigong, dreamwork, meditation, and meaningful cultural exchange, participants will experience Nepal not only as a destination, but as a living path of inner transformation.

From the sacred temples of Kathmandu to the breathtaking Langtang Himal, this pilgrimage combines contemplative practice, ethical adventure, and service to local communities, creating an experience that nourishes body, mind, and spirit.

We are honored to partner with the Karuna Project in offering this extraordinary journey.

Where Your Participation Goes

Paititi Institute’s transformational programs form part of a larger nonprofit ecosystem. Revenue from retreats, courses and educational programs helps sustain the Institute’s conservation, Indigenous education and community initiatives. Participants therefore enter a reciprocal relationship: receiving practices preserved through living wisdom traditions while helping those traditions, communities and landscapes continue into future generations.
